ReelShort vs DramaBox vs NetShort (and ShortMax): which pass to buy

All four work the same way: free first episodes, then coins or a weekly pass. Prices on the US App Store sit in the same range. The real difference is the stories, because each app has its own. Buy where the drama you are hooked on lives, buy one week, and cancel the renewal.

What each app sells on the US App Store

These are the in-app purchases each App Store listing showed on the date at the bottom of this page. The App Store shows a short list, so the apps may sell other plans inside. Prices differ by country and on Android.

  • ReelShort: a $19.99 subscription that its listing says lasts 7 days and renews automatically, plus one-time purchases from $4.99 to $29.99.
  • NetShort: Weekly VIP from $5.99 to $29.99, plus one-time purchases at $9.99 and $19.99.
  • DramaBox: memberships from $5.99 to $19.99 and special offers from $4.99 to $19.99. The listing does not say how long each membership lasts.
  • ShortMax: Weekly Pass Pro at $9.99 and $19.99, plus one-time purchases from $3.49 to $24.99.

  • DramaBox on the App Store (US)
  • NetShort on the App Store (US)
  • ShortMax on the App Store (US)

So which one?

  • Hooked on one story: buy in the app that has it. Catalogs do not overlap.
  • Want variety for a weekend: look at the weekly pass in the one or two apps whose stories you like, buy one, finish, cancel.
  • Just curious: buy nothing yet. Watch the free episodes in each app and see which stories pull you in.

Coins or a pass?

Coins unlock episodes one by one and suit a single story you want to finish. A weekly pass suits a binge across several stories. ReelShort also hands out free coins for check-ins and tasks, which can cover a few episodes.

How not to pay twice

The easy way to overpay is two weekly passes running at once, or one that keeps renewing long after the binge ended. Once a month, open Subscriptions on your phone and cancel anything you are not using.

Cancel on iPhone

  1. Open Settings and tap your name.
  2. Tap Subscriptions.
  3. Tap the drama app's subscription.
  4. Tap Cancel Subscription. If there is no cancel button, or you see an expiration message, it is already cancelled.
  • Apple: cancel a subscription

Cancel on Android

  1. Open Google Play and go to Subscriptions.
  2. Select the drama app's subscription.
  3. Tap Cancel subscription and follow the steps.
  • Google Play: cancel a subscription

Questions

Is ReelShort or DramaBox better?

Neither wins across the board. On the US App Store they sell similar plans in a similar price range; the stories are different. Choose the app that has the drama you want to finish.

Which short drama app is cheapest?

On the US App Store in October 2026, the lowest weekly option we saw was NetShort's $5.99 Weekly VIP, and DramaBox listed a $5.99 membership with no duration stated. The cheapest choice depends on how much you watch that week.

Can I watch short dramas without paying?

Partly. The first episodes are free, and ReelShort gives coins for check-ins and tasks. Finishing a long story usually means paying something.

Do these passes renew automatically?

Weekly passes and subscriptions renew until you cancel. ReelShort's App Store listing says so directly. Cancel in your phone's Subscriptions screen.
